It's the 17th of September, 2026. I decided to put the advice of "just start" into practice and write what will be my first post.

I'll admit, right now, I have no grand idea. A better thought will arise later, and maybe after a few iterations I'll be proud to publish that. But for now, I'm relaxing my editorial standards and just writing. My goal isn't to write well, but just to write. I believe writing is thinking. I don't have to feel a particular way to write i can be sad, happy, inspired believing so makes it feel a lot less scary to sit for 30 min and draft something.

Often, I notice things, trying to connect them to my experiences, only to have the connections dissolve before they become something. I hope this blog becomes a place where those fleeting ideas can linger a little longer, where they get to stay past their usual visit and become something more than a passing thought.
